\documentclass{article}
\title{Federated Scheduling of Appointment Reminders with Privacy Guarantees}
\author{Synthetic Authors (demo data)}
\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Clinics want to schedule appointment reminders without sharing patient histories. We train a scheduler with federated learning so that raw records never leave the clinic.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Missed appointments waste clinic capacity. A reminder policy that adapts to each patient needs data that clinics are reluctant to share.

\section{Method}
Each clinic trains a local model of patient response and shares only model updates. Updates are clipped and noised before they leave the clinic.

\section{Experiments}
We simulate twelve clinics with synthetic patient histories. The federated scheduler is compared with a fixed reminder schedule.

\section{Conclusion}
Federated scheduling keeps records local while improving reminder timing.
